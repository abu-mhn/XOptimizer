What's New

Recent changes to X Optimizer.

Version v1.19.0

  • Tier lists. Library has a new Tier tab that ranks every part S to F by ATK, DEF, STA or weight. Parts that switch mode are listed once per mode.
  • Most played. The Dashboard has its own tier list built from what everyone actually brought to tournaments this month.
  • Side panel choice. On a wide screen, Settings lets you pick what the panel on the right shows: the usual cards, or just the tier list.
  • Opens where it should. On a computer the app now starts on the Calculator, since the side panel already shows the Dashboard. On a phone it still starts on the Dashboard.
