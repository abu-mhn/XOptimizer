What's New

Recent changes to X Optimizer.

Version v1.48.0

  • Settle it with rock-paper-scissors. Tap swap on the scoreboard and pick who plays for the position.
  • Drag combos to swap slots. In the Deck tab, when registering, and at Bey Check.
  • Posters for your event. Add an image when you create a tournament. Tap it to see it full-screen.
  • Date, time and stadium on every card. See where a tournament is before you join it.
  • Hosts can leave without resetting. The tournament keeps running; come back from My Tournaments.
