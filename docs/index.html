What's New

Recent changes to X Optimizer.

Version v1.26.0

  • Battle Royale, simplified. No deck, no cards, no shop for now — just challenge a player and let the Judge call it.
  • Challenge up and down a tier. Not just your own tier any more — S meets A, A meets S and B, and so on.
  • Upsets pay double. Beat someone from a higher tier and you take twice the wager off them.
  • Pick your own Judge. Nobody is chosen for you any more, and there's a search box to find one fast.
  • Sideways prompt in Battle Royale. If your phone won't rotate, it now says why — and you can show the board anyway.
  • Long names fit. Usernames are no longer cut short on the scoreboard.
  • Scrolling rows work on desktop. Rows that slide sideways can now be scrolled with the mouse wheel, or just dragged.
