What's New

Recent changes to X Optimizer.

Version v1.13.0

  • Placement depth. Pick how far a knockout ranks — a Top 8 bracket can decide just 1st to 4th.
  • Removing a player. No longer redraws the tournament. Their opponent gets a free win and everything else stays put.
  • Name changes. Renaming your account keeps your Revox points on the same row instead of starting a new one.
  • Member profiles. Photos, banners and tags now load for everyone on the Revox list.
