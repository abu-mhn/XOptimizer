What's New

Recent changes to X Optimizer.

Version v1.49.0

  • Both of you tap Ready. The scoreboard stays locked until you both say you’re at the table, so nobody can score a battle on their own. Tapped it by mistake? Cancel ready, it costs nothing.
  • No Judge needed. Tap Score this battle — or just turn your phone sideways.
  • Ranking tab in Battle Royale. Everyone by points, with your own place at the top.
  • More on your profile. Where you stand in the Ranking, your Battle Royale points, and your starred deck.
  • Set your standing stake. The most you’ll play for. Each match uses the smaller of the two.
