What's New

Recent changes to X Optimizer.

Version v1.47.0

  • Leave without resetting. Hosts can step away and the tournament keeps running — come back from My Tournaments. Reset is still the one that cancels.
  • The deck check shows your parts. “Is this the deck you’re playing today?” now pictures every slot in full — blade, ratchet and bit.
  • Reorder combos by dragging. Grab a slot’s handle and drop it on another to swap them — in the Deck tab, when registering, and at Bey Check. Arrow keys work too.
  • Give your event a poster. Add an image when you create the tournament and it shows on the card. Tap the poster to see it full-screen.
  • See when and where before you join. Every tournament card — in Open Tournaments and in My Tournaments — shows its date, time, stadium and the host’s remark.
  • Open Tournaments only lists what you can join. Your own tournaments are under My Tournaments instead of in both places.
